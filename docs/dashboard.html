Methodology notes

BSSE-only is misleading for doxorubicin

Without dispersion, counterpoise correction alone drives doxorubicin's binding energy to a near-zero, physically implausible +0.15 kcal/mol — an artifact of omitting π-stacking. D3BJ resolves this.

Internal consistency check

The BSSE magnitude is essentially identical with and without D3BJ (5.87 / 5.87 kcal/mol curcumin, 4.93 / 4.93 kcal/mol doxorubicin) — expected, since BSSE is a pure basis-set artifact independent of dispersion.

BSSE and solvation are not combined

The aqueous correction is reported as D3BJ + SMD(water) on the gas-phase geometry, separate from the BSSE+D3BJ gas-phase estimate — not a five-way stack. Both are valid, complementary refinements of the raw B3LYP number.

Literature divergence, reported honestly

Our aqueous curcumin value (−73.0 kJ/mol) is more negative than a comparable literature figure (−34.24 kJ/mol) — attributed to real methodology differences (cluster size, functional, solvation model), not claimed as agreement.
